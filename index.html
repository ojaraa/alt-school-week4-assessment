About Me

My name is Favour Okereke, and I am a Computer Science graduate with a strong interest in software engineering and building useful digital products. I graduated from the Federal University of Technology, Owerri.

I started developing my practical software engineering skills during my undergraduate internship, where I was introduced to frontend development. Since then, I have continued to build projects using technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, and Next.js.

My long-term goal is to become an AI-focused full-stack engineer capable of building production-ready software products that combine strong frontend experiences with solid backend systems and useful AI capabilities.

Why I Joined AltSchool

I joined the AltSchool School of Engineering because I wanted structured training that would help me move beyond the parts of software development I already understand and build a stronger foundation in full-stack engineering.

The program also provides access to experienced instructors who have vast knowledge of the course and the patience to impart this knowledge, especially for someone like me who has no prior knowledge of technology.

Although I already have experience building frontend applications, I identified several important gaps in my knowledge. I want to become much more comfortable with backend development, databases, APIs, authentication, server-side programming, testing, deployment, and the principles behind designing reliable software systems.

My Goals for the School of Engineering Program

My main goal is to become genuinely competent as a software engineer, rather than simply completing the program. By the end of the program, I want to have a strong understanding of both frontend and backend development and be able to build complete applications from idea to deployment.

My specific goals include:

  1. Strengthen my JavaScript and TypeScript knowledge and understand the language fundamentals more deeply.
  2. Become comfortable building backend applications and working with APIs and databases.
  3. Develop a strong understanding of authentication, authorization, sessions, security, and role-based access control.
  4. Learn how to design and structure applications that are maintainable, reliable, and scalable.
  5. Improve my problem-solving and algorithmic thinking skills.
  6. Become comfortable with developer tools, Git, the command line, testing, deployment, and other parts of the software development workflow.
  7. Build meaningful full-stack projects that demonstrate my engineering ability rather than just my ability to create attractive interfaces.
  8. Eventually combine my full-stack engineering skills with artificial intelligence to build useful AI-powered applications and SaaS products.
  9. Become job-ready for competitive software engineering opportunities locally and internationally.

My long-term goal

My long-term goal is to become an AI Full-Stack Engineer who can independently take a product from an idea to a production-ready application. I want to be able to build products such as AI productivity tools, customer support systems, intelligent dashboards, document and research assistants, workflow automation platforms, and other software products that solve real problems.
